My first draft said the wrong thing.

Source, serum description
Lightweight and fast-absorbing, it layers beautifully under makeup or sunscreen.

Ringan dan cepat terserap, terlapis di bawah cocok dipakai berlapis dengan makeup atau sunscreen.

Nusawarna is a skincare brand I made up to practise localization. Draft one used “terlapis di bawah”, which reversed the meaning, so I rewrote it. The set has five texts, each in its own register: homepage hero, product description, Instagram caption, customer care reply and terms and conditions. Email me for the PDF with source, translation and notes for each.

Source, homepage heroRadiant skin starts with radiant confidence. Nusawarna blends nature’s finest botanicals with modern skincare science, crafted for every Indonesian woman who believes glowing skin is a form of self-love. Discover skincare that listens to your skin, and to you.

Bahasa IndonesiaKulit yang cerah berawal dari kepercayaan diri yang bersinar. Nusawarna memadukan bahan tumbuhan alami terbaik dengan sains perawatan kulit modern, diracik untuk setiap wanita Indonesia yang percaya bahwa kulit yang cerah adalah wujud cinta pada diri sendiri. Temukan skincare yang memahami kulitmu dan dirimu.

Two choices in that piece. English repeats “radiant”, but exact repetition reads flatter in Indonesian, so I used “cerah” and then “bersinar”. And “listens to your skin” became “memahami kulitmu”, because “cocok dengan kulitmu” would say the product suits your skin, which is a different claim.

  • terlibat → digunakan

    I had rendered “introduced” as “terlibat”, which means “involved”.

  • kalian, mereka → kamu

    One informal register across the whole Instagram campaign.

  • berat bersih → isi bersih

    Serum is sold by volume, and “berat bersih” is for weight.
